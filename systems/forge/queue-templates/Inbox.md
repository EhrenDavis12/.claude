# Inbox

> **What this is:** Where you drop new work. Each tick triages what's here — research, a
> path, a size — and moves it to Ready or, if it needs your answer, to Blocked.
> **Yours.** Add lines; the queue removes them as it triages.
> **Hints:** One line per item, plain words, no spec. Anything here *will* be built after
> triage — if you're not ready for that, it belongs in Later. Tag `[prd]` or `[look]` to
> override the triage call.
