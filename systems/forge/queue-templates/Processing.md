# Processing

> started, its branch, and the session working it.
> **The queue's.** Don't edit — a line here means hands off the item and its branch.
> **Hints:** To stop an item, interrupt its session or delete its branch. To steer it, message
> that session. A line whose branch has gone quiet for a day is flagged at session start.
